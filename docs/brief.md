# docs/brief.md — Project Brief

> **INTERNAL ONLY. NEVER PUBLISHED.** This document states commercial and personal objectives that must not appear on the site, in any case study, or in any public artifact.

---

## WHO THIS IS FOR

Moataz Mustapha — senior product designer, Dubai. ~10 years across fintech and digital banking (Mashreq), IoT platforms (IoTBlue), enterprise SaaS, and healthcare. Fine-arts background and an active conceptual art practice alongside the design career.

---

## THE PROBLEM THIS SITE SOLVES

The existing portfolio misrepresents him in three specific ways:

1. **Skill misread.** UI-focused evaluators don't see interface craft; UX-focused evaluators don't see research depth. The work is presented as a process diary rather than as decisions and outcomes.
2. **Seniority invisible.** Case studies read "we" throughout, with no stated role, no owned decisions, and no closed outcomes — which reads as mid-level regardless of actual scope.
3. **One audience, many visitors.** Hiring managers, founders, learners, executives, and cultural evaluators all receive the same undifferentiated presentation.

---

## SUCCESS CRITERIA (measurable, time-bound)

| # | Criterion | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Interview flow** | At least **one interview booked per month**, sustained |
| 2 | **Role level** | Secure a **Lead / Principal / Staff / Head of Design** position |
| 3 | **Residency** | **UAE Golden Visa** (Creatives in Culture & Arts pathway) **by end of year** |

The site is judged against these three outcomes, not against aesthetics or novelty.

---

## THE FIVE AUDIENCES

| Archetype | Who | Their one question | What convinces them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Institution** | Recruiters, hiring managers, design leads, VPs | "Is this person senior, relevant, and safe to advance?" | Outcomes first, role clarity, systems evidence |
| **Founder** | Startup founders, SME clients, PM/CTOs | "Can they take a vague vision and ship it fast?" | Range, velocity, constraint-awareness |
| **Learner** | Students, juniors, career-switchers, Arabic-first designers | "Can this person actually help me?" | Teaching, process visibility, warmth |
| **Leadership** | CEOs, C-suite, investors, journalists, organisers | "Is this a voice worth betting on?" | Philosophy, org-level impact, a quotable thesis |
| **Curator** | Curators, critics, cultural evaluators, collectors | "Is this a coherent, serious practice?" | Concept, documented record, restraint |

MVP-1 serves all five through one classic presentation. Layer 2 introduces the adaptive Door that re-sequences content per archetype.

## POSITIONING

**Domains:** Banking & Identity · Smart Things (IoT) · AI & Conversation · Branding
**Differentiators:** regulated-environment depth (KYC, onboarding, compliance-aware design) · design-systems ownership · bilingual EN/AR with real RTL practice · a designer who builds

**Thesis:** *design means to build, not to draw.*

---

## NON-NEGOTIABLE CONSTRAINTS

1. **No fabricated metrics.** Every figure is labelled projected / achieved / not-measurable. Nothing is invented, ever.
2. **NDA discipline.** Mashreq work appears redacted and abstracted. Internal figures are interview-only, never published.
3. **First person.** "I" throughout. "We" only where the team genuinely owned the outcome.
4. **Every declared target closed.** Achieved, missed, or not-measurable with a reason.
5. **Bilingual is structural.** Not a translation layer bolted on.
6. **Nothing hardcoded.** All content dynamic from day one.
7. **The old site stays live** until the new one is genuinely stronger.

---

## SCOPE POSTURE

**MVP-1 ships the classic portfolio** — gallery plus four case files — built on the complete architecture so nothing is rebuilt later.
**Deferred by design:** the adaptive Door, RAG assistant, articles, comments, Studio, experiments, admin panel, open-source design system.

**Time budget:** ~2 hours/day. **Target:** 6–9 weeks to launch gate.

---

## WHAT WOULD MEAN THIS FAILED

- The site launches and interview volume does not change → the content, not the architecture, was the problem
- Build extends past three months → scope was not cut hard enough
- Any published metric cannot be defended in an interview → the integrity rule was broken
- NDA material appears publicly → a hard failure regardless of any other outcome
